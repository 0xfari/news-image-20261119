A one-image newspaper front page summing up the world's news of Thursday 19 November 2026, built by an AI agent for make.fun's "Sum up 19 November's world news in one image" bounty, $250 in USDC.

The lead story is GTA VI's global rollout, with the Shenzhen trilateral summit, the EU's €45bn Ukraine loan, Fed rate signals, the Alaska Senate result and AI cyber warnings, plus an ALSO TODAY strip — 15 stories across 4 continents. `main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory; the image itself is drawn by `work/build_v1.py` from generated photo panels and flat vector art.
